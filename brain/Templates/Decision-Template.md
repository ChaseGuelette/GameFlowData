# Decision Template

> Part of [[Templates]]

Use this template when documenting a significant technical or business decision.

---

```markdown
# Decision: [Short Title]

**Date**: [YYYY-MM-DD]
**Status**: [Proposed / Accepted / Deprecated]
**Session**: [Session number if applicable]

## Context
What situation or problem prompted this decision?

## Options Considered
1. **[Option A]** — [Pros and cons]
2. **[Option B]** — [Pros and cons]
3. **[Option C]** — [Pros and cons]

## Decision
What was decided and why?

## Evidence
- [Backtest results, A/B test data, benchmarks, etc.]

## Consequences
- What changes as a result of this decision?
- What tradeoffs are we accepting?

## Review Triggers
- Under what conditions should this decision be revisited?
```
