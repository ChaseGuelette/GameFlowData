# Target Audience

> Part of [[Business]]

## Primary: Quantitative Sports Bettors
- Use data and models to find edges, not gut feelings
- Care about: ROI proof, model transparency, edge quantification, line shopping
- Already familiar with: expected value, Kelly criterion, vig removal, prop betting
- Active on: Discord, Twitter/X sports betting communities, betting forums
- Willing to pay for tools that demonstrably improve their edge

## Secondary: DFS Players
- Daily fantasy sports players on PrizePicks, Underdog, etc.
- Care about: finding +EV lines, comparing platforms, building slips
- The DFS Edge Finder serves this audience WITHOUT requiring model predictions
- Market edge (devigged consensus vs DFS lines) works for all 6 stats

## Who We're NOT For
- Casual fans who want "lock of the day" picks
- People looking for guaranteed wins
- Users who don't understand probability and expected value

## Value Proposition
"Model-driven probability distributions for player props, not just picks. Know your edge before you bet."
