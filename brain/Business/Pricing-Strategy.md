# Pricing Strategy

> Part of [[Business]]

## Current Model
Free beta — no paywall. Public `/picks` page shows 3 real picks (blurred rest). Full access after sign-up.

## Target Market
Quantitative sports bettors and DFS players who want model-driven edges. NOT casual fans.

## What Users Get
- Daily model predictions with probability distributions (Q10-Q90)
- Edge quantification against sharpest available lines
- AI-powered pick analysis (Claude Haiku)
- DFS edge finder across 6 stats and all major platforms
- Line shopping by state/book
- Kelly sizing recommendations
- Cross-device bet tracking with performance analytics
- Data Vault with player/team/defense heatmap tables

## Pricing Considerations
- Sports betting tools market ranges from $30-200/mo
- Key differentiator: probabilistic modeling (not just "picks") + transparent edge quantification
- ROI proof is the strongest marketing asset (63% hit rate, ~29% ROI in backtests)
- Consider tiered access (basic picks vs full analysis + DFS + AI chat)

## Competitive Landscape
Research needed on: OddsJam, Unabated, PicksWise, ActionNetwork, BettingPros, LineStarApp
