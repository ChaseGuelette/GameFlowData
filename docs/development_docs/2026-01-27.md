- **`src/models/analyze_calibration_drift.py`** (new): Standalone analysis tool that runs minutes-rate correlation analysis and combined calibration checks against an existing model without retraining.
- **`src/models/train_pipeline.py`**: Added two new methods to the training pipeline:

  - `_analyze_minutes_rate_correlation()` - Computes correlation between actual minutes and per-minute rates by bucket, flags stats with significant correlation.

### 3. Monte Carlo Prediction Improvements (`src/models/monte_carlo.py`)

Four new inference-time mechanisms added to `MonteCarloPredictor`:

| Feature | Purpose | Default |
|---------|---------|---------|
| **Correlated Sampling** | Adjusts rate samples based on minutes samples to capture minutes-rate correlation | Enabled for PTS (corr=0.327), AST (corr=0.184) |
| **Variance Inflation** | Widens distribution around median for high-variance stats | REB: 1.15x |
| **Tail Adjustment** | Extends extrapolated tails more aggressively | Lower tail: 1.15x |
| **Blowout/Foul Factor** | Mixture model that reduces minutes in ~5% of samples | Disabled by default |

Also added `compute_correlation_config_from_data()` utility function to derive correlation factors from any training dataset.

## Decisions Made

1. **Correlated sampling over variance inflation for PTS/AST**: Analysis showed PTS has +0.327 correlation between minutes and rate (rate goes from 0.338 at 10-15 min to 0.581 at 40+ min). This is a bias problem, not a variance problem, so correlation adjustment is the correct fix. See `analysis_results.json` for raw data.

2. **Variance inflation for REB only**: REB has near-zero correlation (-0.039) so correlated sampling doesn't help. Variance inflation (1.15x) is the right tool here. See correlation analysis output.

3. **Blowout factor disabled by default**: When enabled at 8% probability / 35% reduction, it over-corrected middle quantiles (PTS Q50 went from -2.3% to -5.6%). Set to disabled with conservative defaults (5% / 30%) for manual tuning.

4. **Return on capital vs ROI on stakes**: Kept both metrics. ROI on stakes is standard in sports betting (efficiency measure). Return on capital is what matters for bankroll management. Both are now in output and labeled clearly.

5. **No retraining required**: All Monte Carlo fixes are inference-time adjustments. The underlying minutes and rate models are unchanged. Backtest can be re-run immediately.

## Problems / Blockers

- **REB Q25/Q50 still has 5-7% gaps**: Variance inflation at 1.15x improved tails (Q10, Q90) but the middle quantiles suggest a systematic bias in the rebounds model. May need higher inflation (1.25x) or a different approach entirely.
- **AST Q10 has +8.7% over-coverage**: The model's floor for assists is too high. Tail adjustment and blowout factor help but don't fully solve it. The assists distribution may be fundamentally non-gaussian (lots of 0-1 assist games).
- **Tuning parameter interactions**: The four mechanisms interact - blowout factor shifts distribution down while correlation shifts it up. Finding optimal settings requires iterative backtest runs.

## Open Questions / Risks

1. **Are the default correlation factors stable across seasons?** They were derived from 2024-2025 data. If the minutes-rate relationship shifts season to season, the hardcoded defaults could introduce new bias. The `compute_correlation_config_from_data()` function exists to recompute from fresh data.
2. **Should we train a direct total-stat model?** The two-stage (minutes x rate) architecture inherently loses information about the joint distribution. A single model predicting total PTS directly would avoid the multiplication problem entirely, but loses the interpretability of minutes + rate.
3. **Is the 51.7% backtest win rate real edge or noise?** Need to break even at ~52.4% with typical -110 vig. The calibration fixes should improve edge estimation accuracy, but we won't know until running a new backtest.
4. **Should correlation factors be recomputed during each training run?** Currently hardcoded as defaults. Could add `compute_correlation_config_from_data()` call to the training pipeline so they update automatically.
