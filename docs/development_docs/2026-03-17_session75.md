## What Was Done

### 1. Calibration A/B Backtest

**Context:** Production model (`run_20260210_095220`, 35 days old) showed declining performance — ROI dropped from 16.8% to 4.1%, PTS ECE nearly 2x threshold at 0.097.

**Process:**
1. Copied production model to `production_recal/`
2. Generated fresh conformal offsets using `--calibrate-only` (16,308 rows, 2,000 MC samples, Season 22025 through Mar 1)
3. Ran parallel backtests (Mar 1-16): baseline (no offsets) vs recalibrated (with offsets)
4. Initial runs had SSL timeouts — only 1 day of lines data (21 bets). Fixed DB issues, reran with full data (328 bets).

**Results (Mar 1-16, 16 days, full data):**

| Metric | Baseline (no offsets) | Recalibrated (with offsets) | Delta |
|--------|----------------------|---------------------------|-------|
| Total Bets | 328 | 311 | -17 |
| Win Rate | 71.0% | 69.5% | -1.6pp |
| ROI | 35.2% | 22.4% | **-12.8pp** |
| Sharpe | 2.50 | 1.63 | -0.87 |
| Max Drawdown | 21.0% | 44.2% | +23.2pp |

REB ROI cratered from 43.4% to 11.4% (-32pp). Offsets shift quantile predictions upward, destroying the under-bet edge.

**Decision:** DO NOT DEPLOY offsets. This is the 3rd independent confirmation (Session 42, Feb 18 retrain, now this).

## Key Findings

### The Calibration Paradox (Confirmed 3x)

The model's lower-quantile "miscalibration" (Q25/Q50 under-coverage for PTS/REB) is not a bug — it's what generates the under-betting edge. The conformal offsets shift predictions upward to close coverage gaps, but this removes the systematic bias toward under bets that makes the model profitable.

**Rule established:** NEVER deploy global conformal calibration offsets. Future experiments limited to targeted single-stat, single-quantile adjustments only.

### Incremental Retraining Works

Adding the current season to training data (22025 → 3 seasons total) with locked hyperparams improved performance without the risks of a full retrain with new hyperparams. The Feb 18 retrain failure was due to re-tuning hyperparams, not the concept of retraining.
